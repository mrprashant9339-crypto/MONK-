MONK Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-11 · Effective 11 November 2026

This policy describes the categories of information MONK processes to provide its workspace services for education institutions and businesses.

Information processed

Depending on your use, MONK may process account identifiers and profile details, organization memberships and roles, workspace content, invitations, activity and audit records, and technical diagnostics.

Purposes

Information is used to authenticate users, operate organization workspaces, enforce permissions, deliver requested features, maintain security, troubleshoot issues and meet legal obligations.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by infrastructure providers engaged to host and operate MONK, including the configured database and application hosting providers. MONK does not needlessly expose service credentials to browser clients.

Retention and security

Information is retained as needed to provide the service and meet legal requirements. Technical and organizational safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal information through the MONK operator. Organization administrators may control workspace records subject to legal requirements.

Contact and review

Use the contact channel published by the MONK operator for privacy requests. This baseline policy must be completed with the operator’s legal identity, contact details, retention periods and jurisdiction-specific disclosures before public commercial launch.
